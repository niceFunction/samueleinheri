THIS IS ME

As a Game Designer specializing in System Design, I thrive on designing emergent systems that challenge players, fuel their curiosity, and keep them hooked from beginning to end.

I believe in a future where everyone can play a game, which is why I champion Game Accessibility by designing inclusive experiences and participating in efforts to shape industry-wide standards.

During my free time, I spend time helping students and juniors with their resumes and portfolios, so far, I've supported well over 200 people, and it's something I really enjoy doing.
